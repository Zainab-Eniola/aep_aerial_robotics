"Reckoning" is a way of calculating something, a view point or status.

Dead reckoning is the method used to calculate the current position of a moving object by using previously determined position (or last known location),  and incorporating estimates of speed, direction, and elapsed time.

HOW DEAD RECKONING WORKS
1. Say you start from a known fixed point on the map, keep track of heading and speed (monitoring direction and speed over a period of time) the distance can be calculated.
2. Three accelerometers measure the changes in speed and motion(acceleration/deceleration) across three dimensions. The accelerometers must overcome the gravity trap (sometimes upward tilt causes gravity to pull backward on the sensors, tricking the sensors to confuse tilt with upward acceleration).
3. Gyroscopes use spinning wheels to maintain a fixed spatial orientation and stabilize the internal senser platform.
4. Three gimbals: A mechanical ring setup allowing the center platform to rotate freely, the outer gimbal handles roll (side-to-side tilt), the middle gimbal handles pitch (up-and-down nose tilt), and lastly, the inner circle handles yaw (left or right turn). The gimbals must remain perfectly flat relative to the horizon regardless of how the aircraft maneuvers. 

Some of the advantages include; 
1. Independence: Some of the devices that enable this technology are completely independent of the physical/electromagnetic world. They do not need radio towers, or signals).This makes it reliable for GPS-Denied environments.
2. Immunity to jamming.
3. High update rates.
